Yelp brand assets. Do not edit, recolor, redraw or stretch these files.

What they are
  yelp_logo.svg   The Yelp logo, light-background version.
                  From https://www.yelp.com/brand (yelp_logos.zip: Logo/Light bg/RGB).
  stars/          Yelp's branded star ratings, desktop, 20 px high, @1x and @2x.
                  From https://terms.yelp.com/developers/display_requirements/
                  (ReviewRibbon_v2.zip: Desktop/medium_20).
  Both were downloaded 2026-09-24 and are used unaltered.

Why they are here
  HaulQ's repair-shop search shows Yelp ratings. Yelp's display requirements say
  the Yelp logo must be prominently visible on content that came from Yelp, that
  a Yelp rating must be shown with Yelp's own branded stars ("generic or
  unbranded stars are not permitted"), next to the review count, and that each
  business links to its own Yelp page. Yelp data must not be stored longer than
  24 hours. HaulQ stores none of it.

Rules for anyone changing this
  - Yelp owns the logo and the stars. Do not use them anywhere that is not Yelp
    content, and do not use them to suggest Yelp endorses HaulQ.
  - Questions about use: api@yelp.com (display) and trademarks@yelp.com (brand).

File names for the stars follow Yelp's own scheme. The half-star names are not
obvious: _half is 0.5, _2_1_half is 1.5 and _2_half is 2.5.
